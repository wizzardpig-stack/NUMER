Privacy & Method

Names, birth dates, archived plates, settings, and calculations stay in this browser via local storage. NUMER does not send entered identity data to a server. Google-hosted font files may be requested by the page, but your entered data is not included in those requests.

Interpretive Scope

NUMER implements a Pythagorean numerology model as a symbolic reflection tool. Relationship scores, coherence, archetypal descriptions, and developmental language are interpretive model outputs, not scientific measurements or predictions.

System

Pythagorean A-Z mapping. Master numbers 11, 22, and 33 are retained according to the engine rules. Calculation traces remain visible so every primary result can be audited.
